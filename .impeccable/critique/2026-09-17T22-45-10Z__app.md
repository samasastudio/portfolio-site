## Overall Impression

High-craft, uncompromising editorial aesthetic with genuine physical presence. Completely avoids AI-generated blandness. Primary risks: desktop viewport locking (`100dvh` + `overflow: hidden`) causing silent truncation on scaled laptops, sub-accessible 6px–7px micro-typography, and missing WAI-ARIA tab semantics.

## What's Working

1. **Physical Permanence & Anti-Slop Discipline**: Washi paper stocks, sumi ink hairlines, and 0px blur hard-offset shadows give the interface tactile weight, rejecting the ephemeral gloss of modern boilerplate.
2. **Typographic Voice**: The friction between dense sans display headers, Georgia serif italics, and monospace coordinates creates an authentic, memorable authorial tone.
3. **Restrained Focal Color**: Vermilion Cinnabar (#b64c31) and Ochre Gold (#d6a934) are strictly throttled to $\le 10\%$ surface area, preserving the punch of interactive stamps.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Desktop Viewport Locking & Clipping on Constrained Displays (<720px Height / 125%–150% Scaling)**
  - **Why it matters**: `body { overflow: hidden; }` and `.site { height: 100dvh; overflow: hidden; }` completely amputate the bottom of the screen on 1080p laptops at 125%–150% scaling or windows with devtools open. Hero indices, project card metrics, and footers become unreachable.
  - **Fix**: In `app/globals.css`, remove `overflow: hidden` from `body`. Update `.site` to `min-height: 100dvh; height: auto;` or add `overflow-y: auto` to `.stage` on short viewports.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

- **[P1] Sub-Accessible Micro-Type Scale (6px–7px Monospace Labels)**
  - **Why it matters**: 6px and 7px text across `.shelf`, `.brand-caption`, `.navrail small`, `.eyebrow`, `.hero-index`, and `.ticker` fails WCAG AA legibility criteria on standard DPI screens, rendering as unreadable smudges.
  - **Fix**: Elevate micro-label font size floor to 9px or 10px (`text-[9px]` or `text-[10px]`). Maintain the delicate archival index aesthetic via letter spacing (`tracking-[0.14em]`) and muted ink opacity (`opacity-65`) rather than microscopic physical point sizes.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`

- **[P2] Incomplete WAI-ARIA Tab Implementation in Project Archive**
  - **Why it matters**: `ProjectArchive.tsx` declares `role="tablist"` and `role="tab"` but lacks `aria-controls`, `aria-labelledby`, `role="tabpanel"`, and keyboard arrow navigation (`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Home`/`End`). Screen readers and keyboard-only users cannot cycle projects.
  - **Fix**: Implement roving `tabIndex` and keyboard arrow navigation handler in `ProjectArchive.tsx`. Bind tabs to the project card container with `role="tabpanel"` and matching IDs.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Hardcoded White & Color System Drift**
  - **Why it matters**: `rgba(255,255,255,.42)` in `.round-link::after` breaks *The No-Faux-White Rule*. Low contrast on Sumi Pine Moss (`--moss` #67745a against washi paper yields 4.04:1, failing AA for small text).
  - **Fix**: Replace hardcoded white with `rgba(250,245,234,.42)` (`var(--paper-light)`). Darken `--moss` token to `#4e5d42` to hit $\ge 4.5:1$ contrast.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable colorize`

- **[P3] Gateway Surfaces Disconnected from Primary Navigation Rail**
  - **Why it matters**: `PRODUCT.md` designates the site as the primary frontend gateway for 3 live agentic consoles (`/systems/scraper`, `/systems/graphical-atlas`, `/systems/generative-ui`), but `navigationItems` in `_data/navigation.ts` only lists routes `01` through `04`. Systems remain hidden.
  - **Fix**: Add a dedicated `05 Systems` item to `navigationItems` with an status tag or introduce a systems shelf component.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`
